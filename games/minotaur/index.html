The Minotaur

Deep beneath Crete lies the Labyrinth, and something big is walking its halls.
Gather every strand of golden thread to open the gate, then escape. Get caught and you drop a thread and get flung somewhere else in the maze.

Joy-Con grip
🎮 Using a controller: move with the stick or d-pad to pick an option, press A to choose it. Press + to jump straight in.
In the maze: hold B X Y or ZR to sprint · A throws a fireball (once you've found one) · L or R switches the camera · + or − pauses.
Two Joy-Cons showing as one controller? That's normal in Chrome. In 2-player mode the game splits it: left Joy-Con = Theseus (sprint L, fireball ZL), right Joy-Con = Ariadne (move with stick or the four buttons, sprint R, fireball ZR).
Every game builds a brand-new random labyrinth. You only see what you've explored, and the map in the corner fills in as you go. Once the last thread is found, the Minotaur hears the gate open and comes for you.
Boss levels ⚔️ come after every 3 mazes. Make the Great Minotaur crash into a pillar, then hit him with a fireball from the braziers while he's dazed. Here (and only here) you have hearts; lose them all and you're a ghost until you touch a teammate.
Controllers / Joy-Cons: pair over Bluetooth, then press any button. Controller 1 is Theseus (blue), controller 2 is Ariadne (purple). − or + pauses.
Keyboard: Player 1 arrows (sprint: Enter or Right Shift, fireball: M or Right Ctrl), Player 2 W A S D (sprint: Left Shift or Q, fireball: F), Player 3 I J K L (sprint U, fireball O), Player 4 number pad 8 4 5 6 (sprint 7, fireball 9). C switches camera.
3 or 4 players: Perseus (green) and Atalanta (orange) join in. They need a controller or a share of the keyboard, since the on-screen arrows only cover players 1 and 2.
Trader chests ✨ appear by magic around the labyrinth (look for the purple marks on the map). Open one to get a fireball: throw it at the Minotaur to stun him for a few seconds.
Controllers: stick or d-pad to move, hold a face button or ZR to sprint, L or R to switch camera.
Levers: step on a glowing lever to open the gate of the same colour. It only stays open for a few seconds, so run, or have your partner hold it!
Touchscreen: on-screen arrows. Lost? 📷 shows the whole maze.
Camera (📷, C, or L/R) cycles Close → Map → First person. In first person, up walks forward, down steps back, left and right turn. With 2 players the screen splits in two.
